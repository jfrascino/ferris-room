# Ferris Bueller's Bedroom — 3D diorama (work in progress)

An interactive 3D recreation of Ferris Bueller's bedroom from *Ferris Bueller's Day Off* (1986), rebuilt item by item from film frames.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click an object to see what it is and which film frames it was matched to. Switch scenes (opening, dummy, finale, morning), lighting and views from the panel.

Fan project, not affiliated with Paramount. Posters, album art and other artwork belong to their respective owners and appear here only to recreate the set.
